Cashback credit cards offer a percentage of the spending back as cash rewards. It essentially means savings on every rupee spent. The 50% Jewels on shopping merchants are now split: 5% is credited instantly on each transaction and the remaining 45% is credited at billing cycle-end only if qualified spend in that cycle is greater than ₹15,000, otherwise the held 45% is cancelled. Rent, utilities, fuel, insurance, wallet and gift card loads, tax and government payments and EMI transactions do not count toward the ₹15,000 even though they raise your statement total.
